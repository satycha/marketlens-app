    Features

    Today's briefing

    People reaching each step (build 32 and later).

    News stories

    Stories opened, per person and story.

    Pulse videos

    Videos shown, per person and video.

    Explore ideas

    Ideas opened, per person and idea, plus first-try checks.

    Walks

    Walks started, per person and walk. Stories count when heard to the end (build 38 and later; earlier builds counted a story when it started).

    Companies

    Company pages opened, per person and company (build 37 and later for the steps).

    Content

    Lesson reports and suggestions

    Open items for review, oldest first: problems reported on lesson pages, better examples and missing ideas. Set a status in the database once handled.

    Story problem reports

    Open reports on News stories, oldest first. A decision applies to every open report of that story, and reporters see it under My reports.

    Walk reports

    Route checks and problems reported on walks, newest first. Includes team accounts, since owners field-test routes.
