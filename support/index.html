MarketLens Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email marketlensapp@gmail.com and we'll reply within two business days.

Common questions

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap "Forgot password?". We'll email you a link. Open it on your iPhone to set a new password.

How do I delete my account? Tap your initials at the top right of any tab to open You, then choose Delete account at the bottom. This permanently deletes your account, profile answers and usage history.

How do I stop sharing usage data? Tap your initials at the top right to open You. Under Settings, turn off "Share usage data". The app works the same either way.

I found a mistake in a story. Open the story, swipe to its last slide, and tap "Report a problem with this story". Stories reported by several people are hidden until we review them.

Is MarketLens investment advice? No. MarketLens is for learning only. It explains the news and the finance ideas behind it, but it never tells you what to buy or sell.

Why does a walk need my location? Walks tell each stop's story as you reach it. Your location stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

Where do the stories come from? MarketLens reads headlines from major news outlets and public economic data, then writes its own explanation with the help of AI. Each story links to the original reports.
