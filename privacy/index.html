MarketLens Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

MarketLens ("we", "us") is an app that explains financial news and the ideas behind it. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. MarketLens is operated by Satyam Chauhan, reachable at marketlensapp@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You sign up with an email address and password, or with Apple, Google, Facebook or LinkedIn where offered. Questions about you during sign-up are optional.
  • We record how you use the app (for example, which stories you read and for how long) so we can improve it.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your location, used only for walking tours, is never sent to us.
  • You can turn off usage data at any time, and you can delete your account and its data from Settings.

What we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and password. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our database provider; we cannot see it.
  • If you sign in with Apple, Google, Facebook or LinkedIn: the email address and name that service shares with us, and an account identifier from it. We never receive your password for that service, and we don't post to it or read your contacts. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email; Apple then gives us a private relay address.

Optional profile answers. If you answer these during sign-up, we keep:

  • your first and last name
  • the year you were born (to understand age groups and to confirm you are 13 or older)
  • what describes you (for example, student or professional)
  • your school or employer, and field of study
  • your finance experience, your goals and the topics you're interested in
  • how you heard about MarketLens
  • whether you'd like update emails

We also record your country (from your device's region setting) and time zone.

How you use the app. We record:

  • which screens, stories, chapters, concepts, companies, articles, walks and videos you open, and how long you spend on them
  • your 👍/👎 ratings, "Was this helpful?" answers and problem reports
  • company searches you type
  • sign-in and onboarding steps

Each event includes:

  • your device model, iOS version, app version, language and time zone
  • a random ID created for your installation of the app
  • a random ID for each session

Location (walking tours only). When you start walking a route, the app uses your location to tell each stop's story as you reach it and to show walking directions to the next stop. Location is processed on your phone and is not sent to us. To draw walking directions, your phone asks Apple Maps for a route, which shares your approximate position with Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Listening to a walk from anywhere doesn't use your location. Where you are on a walk (which stop, which stories you've heard) is saved on your phone only, so you can continue later.

Videos (the Pulse tab). Pulse plays short videos from news channels on YouTube, using YouTube's embedded player. MarketLens uses YouTube API Services to find these videos. When you watch one, the player is loaded from YouTube, so YouTube (Google) receives your device's IP address and information about your device and viewing, and may set cookies, under the Google Privacy Policy. Use of these videos is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. We don't send YouTube or Google any of your MarketLens account information, and we don't receive any information about your YouTube or Google account. On our side, we record which videos you open and how long you watch, like the rest of the app.

Your Scorecard and saved companies. The Scorecard on the You page (your weekly plan, which ideas and stories you've explored, your answers to check questions, and your activity), the companies you save in Companies with any question you write about them, the companies you've opened recently, the Pulse videos you save (only the video's ID and MarketLens's own question and idea for it), your learning preferences (learning goals and any deadlines you set, explanation depth and session length), the suggestions you mark "Not for me" (an idea or a subject), any notes you write on walk stops, ideas ("My explanation"), capstones, interview practice (on a company or a news story), a company brief's conclusion or at a point in a Pulse video (the video's ID, the second and your note), questions you save as still unclear (with the idea or video point they came from), walk plans you save (a day and your note), capstone plans (start and finish dates), a topic list you paste in (your topics, the ideas they matched and any finish date you set) and whether you remembered your own explanations in Review are kept on your phone and saved to your account, so they're still there on a new phone or after reinstalling. They're used only to show you your own progress, your saved companies and suggestions. They're saved whether or not you share usage data. Problem reports you send about a lesson (what kind, and any text you add) are stored with your account so you can see their outcome under My lesson reports; deleting your account deletes them. Results reminders you turn on for a company, a weekly reminder about your open questions and reminders for a capstone plan are scheduled by your phone and never sent to MarketLens. News stories you save are kept only on your phone (their headline and line), not in your account. "Export my learning record" in You makes a readable copy on your phone that you can share; MarketLens doesn't receive it. "Reset learning progress" in You clears them, and deleting your account deletes them.

What we don't collect: your contacts, photos, advertising identifier, precise location, or any information about your own investments or financial accounts.

How we use it

  • To run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To personalize what you see, for example stories on topics you chose.
  • To understand which stories and lessons help people learn, and to fix what doesn't work.
  • To send you update emails, only if you opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores your account, profile and usage data, and handles sign-in. Data is stored in the United States.
  • Resend delivers sign-in, confirmation and password emails, so it receives your email address and the email it sends.
  • Apple, Google, Meta (Facebook) and LinkedIn confirm who you are when you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies cover what they do.
  • YouTube (Google) provides the videos in Pulse and plays them in its own player. See "Videos" above; the Google Privacy Policy applies.
  • OpenAI generates the explanations in the app. It receives news headlines, public economic data and lesson text, not your personal information.
  • News and data providers supply headlines and public data. Some public data (company filings from the SEC, U.S. Treasury and Bureau of Labor Statistics data, and prices from Yahoo Finance) is requested directly by your phone, so those services see your device's IP address, as with any web request. No account or profile information is sent to them.
  • Apple distributes the app (App Store and TestFlight) under its own privacy policy.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or others.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and profile while your account exists. Usage data is kept for up to 24 months and then deleted or made anonymous. When you delete your account, your profile, your Scorecard and all usage data linked to it are deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off usage data: You → Settings → "Share usage data". The app works the same either way.
  • Delete your account: You → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile answers, Scorecard and usage history.
  • Access or correct your data: email marketlensapp@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as to object to processing or to receive a copy of your data. Contact us to use them.

Children

MarketLens is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to our database is restricted, and each user can read only their own account information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
